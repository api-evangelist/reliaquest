Architecture in one pass — GreyMatter is six layers that work as one platform: (1) Agentic
Orchestration, the plain-language operating layer over every connected technology; (2) Agentic
Teammates, six purpose-built agentic personas; (3) the Context Layer, where optional products
extend the platform; (4) the AI Model Broker, which selects the best model per task; (5) the
patented Universal Translator, which normalizes any source to OCSF in real time without storing
it; and (6) 300+ Technology Connections into the customer's existing stack. No rip-and-replace.

## Agentic AI Teammates

Six purpose-built agentic personas, each leveraging hundreds of agent skills and tools to work
toward an objective, not just discrete tasks. Built on 10+ years of proprietary incident response
data. They augment human analysts rather than replace them.

- [GreyMatter Agentic Teammates](https://reliaquest.com/security-operations-platform/greymatter-agentic-teammates/): Overview of all six personas and how they coordinate.
- IR Analyst: Automatically investigates and responds to 100% of alerts across 300+ technologies with 99.4% accuracy, more than 78M times a year. Collects and stitches data from every tool, enriches with asset info and threat intel, retains organizational context, and executes risk-adjusted response playbooks.
- Detection Engineer: Creates, tunes, and deploys detections from natural-language input. Builds and tunes any rule across any technology from a chat window, automatically tests and deploys detection logic, and analyzes coverage gaps.
- Threat Hunter: Runs hunts across 300+ technologies in natural language, using custom or pre-built hunt packages, then analyzes results and generates reports.
- Threat Intel Analyst: Produces high-fidelity threat intelligence connecting external risk to the internal environment. Aggregates 60+ threat feeds across the open, deep, and dark web and tailors reporting to the customer's environment and industry.
- IT Engineer: Autonomously keeps tools online and issues offline. Monitors and triages infrastructure-health alerts and takes automated action to restore service and reduce downtime.
- OT Engineer: Brings OT and IT environments together to deliver decision-ready investigations while keeping humans in control of every OT action. Correlates OT events with IT identity, endpoint, email, and VPN data.
